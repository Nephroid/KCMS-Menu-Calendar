Enterprise IT Integration Workflow

Configuring the Epson LS300 into a "Dumb TV" commercial monitor for Google TV Streamer / PC pass-through.

1

Enable "Basic TV" Mode

During initial setup (or after factory reset), choose Basic TV instead of Google TV. This strips out forced Google accounts, smart TV ad tracking, and OS bloatware.

2

Set Power-On Input

Navigate to Settings > System > Power & Energy > Power On Behavior and select "Last Input Used". The projector will bypass all internal menus on startup.

3

Plug in Google TV Streamer

Connect your company **Google TV 4K Box** (or presentation PC) directly into **HDMI 1**. Powering on the streaming box remote instantly wakes the projector to HDMI 1.
